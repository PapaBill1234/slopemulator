# Card ld381-r2: LobbyDaemon account inventory link and the item jobs

## Goal
LobbyDaemon has an account inventory link (our protocol) and runs the goods jobs and the 7 item-service jobs to their end. Without a configured link, the jobs keep the original "off" codes of today.

## Source rule (user decision, 2026-11-12)

| 381 logic that differs from 2019 and the client can see it | 381 wins |

## Work
1. Packet crate: new module `account_inventory_link.rs` with `Message` (Hello, the 23 replies of `02-links.md` lines 146–168, and the requests). Add gamed variants 56, 57/58/59, 60/61/62, 63/64/65, 66/67/68, 69/70/71 to `gamed_link.rs`.
2. `src/account_inventory.rs`: trait `AccountInventory` (see "Interfaces"), `OffAccountInventory` (every call `Err(7)`, today's behaviour), `LinkAccountInventory` (one outbound link, transaction map, timeout = link limit, reconnect hook for `ld381-r3`).
3. `main.rs`: `--account-inventory <addr:port>`. Without it, use `OffAccountInventory`.
4. Goods jobs 64/67/70/73/76: send the request, map Ok/Fail to the LobbyGate Ok/Fail of `14-jobs-b.md` §3 and `15-jobs-c.md` §1. Fragmented Ok (8, 11, 14) is one reply list.
5. Item jobs 82–100: the state machine of `17-jobs-e.md` lines 7–23 and `19-jobs-g.md` §1, §2, §5. Each step is one `async` call on the job copy (decision 2). Reply codes: `17-jobs-e.md` lines 34–57. The service entry rows go to the database at each step (Store methods below), so a restart can find them (`LoadIncompleteLobbyService`).
6. `DeleteCharacterJob` goods step (`16-jobs-d.md` lines 139–150) when `check-character-goods-for-character-deletion` is on.
7. `GetServiceEntryListReq` (79): list the open service entries of the session (`FetchServiceEntryList`), format `hiqCqQqohq;`.
8. Store: the 31 r2 wrappers of `25-rust-coverage.md` §9 in `Store`, `SyncStore`, `MemoryStore`, `MssqlStore`.
9. `Settings`: the 18 r2 keys of `25-rust-coverage.md` §10 and `use-account-inventory`.
10. Write the result into `25-rust-coverage.md` (r2 rows).
